Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal data required to provide our services: usernames, email addresses (if provided), and your test performance metrics.

2. Data Usage & Sharing

Your data is used exclusively to track your progress, manage your account, and generate the performance analytics seen in your dashboard. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with essential service providers (e.g., cloud hosting) necessary for the platform to function.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and authentication state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

4. Data Storage & Retention

We use secure cloud storage to ensure your study progress is saved across sessions. We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate personal information.

To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
